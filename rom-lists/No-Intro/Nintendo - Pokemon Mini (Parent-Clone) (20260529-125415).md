## Approach

A broad introduction to a tiny library, centred on puzzles and short play sessions. Nearly all the original commercial games remain: the useful curation decisions are choosing readable releases, avoiding demonstration fragments and identifying the one stronger language barrier.

These are simple games designed for a very small screen, not miniature versions of the main Pokémon RPGs. Their appeal lies in compact challenges and unusual ideas rather than long campaigns. The reduction statistic includes regional copies, previews and extracted minigames, not 46 distinct commercial games.

## Release choices

- Prefer English USA/shared Western releases when available. Japanese exclusives remain eligible for predominantly visual action and puzzles, with instructions or a guide where needed.
- **Pokémon Tetris** represents the regional game titled **Pokémon Shock Tetris** in Japan. They are not two separate selections. The European entry explicitly includes English.
- **Pokémon Zany Cards** represents Pokémon Anime Card Daisakusen. Japanese, French and German copies are not additional games in this collection.
- The full **Party Mini** and **Pichu Bros. Mini** cartridges take precedence over separate previews or extracted versions of their constituent minigames.
- **Puzzle Collection Vol. 2** is a separate game with different content, not a regional alternative to the first collection.
- Keep the exact unaccented DAT filenames and `.min` extensions. **Togepi no Daibouken** is the selected original release commonly described in English as Togepi's Great Adventure.
- No BIOS image, preview, demo, unofficial release or fan translation is silently included. Japanese imports remain their original retail ROMs.

## Language and controls

**Pokémon Sodateyasan Mini**, commonly called Breeder Mini, is held for a deliberate Japanese-language decision. Its creature-care simulation makes menus and instructions more central than in the selected reflex and spatial games. This is an accessibility hold, not a judgement that it is a poor game.

The Japanese selections are not assumed to be entirely language-free. Pichu's minigames and the second Puzzle Collection may need rule references; Race Mini and Togepi may also need help with menus and objectives. Existing fan translations are separate editions, not replacements hidden behind retail filenames.

The original machine has A, B and C buttons plus a shock sensor. Preserve access to the **Shake** action where a game uses it; a handheld does not need to be physically shaken when that action is mapped to a button. Rumble can convey prompts, so those cues should remain perceptible through vibration or an appropriate visual effect. Optional infrared multiplayer is not assumed to be available.

## Scope

Snorlax's Lunch Time is a separate GameCube-distributed novelty, not automatically another full cartridge game for this selection. Likewise, Pokemon Channel demonstrations and extracted minigame variants do not expand the count simply because they have individual DAT entries.

Mini Running Ant and Silver Falls: Monsters In North Island are held for a separate homebrew expansion. The purpose here is a coherent introduction to the original commercial library, not a rule to collect every program that runs on the hardware.
