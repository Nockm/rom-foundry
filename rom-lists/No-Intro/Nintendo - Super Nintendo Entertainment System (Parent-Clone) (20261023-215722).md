## Editorial approach

A broad collection worth exploring, rather than just a ranking of acclaimed games. Established classics sit alongside good genre games, worthwhile licensed releases, unusual mechanics, cult favourites and historically interesting adaptations. A fixed game count was not the selection rule.

The research sources below provided recommendation pools and cross-checks, supplemented by editorial nominations. This is not a claim that every game has multiple independent endorsements, that every adaptation is the best way to play that game, or that each has been tested on the handheld. Inclusion can reflect discovery value despite rough edges.

Coverage includes platforming and exploration, cinematic action, beat-'em-ups, fighters, scrolling shooters, RPGs, strategy and simulation, puzzles, pinball, racing and sports. Examples of discovery nominations include Skyblazer, Firestriker, Operation Logic Bomb, The Firemen, Robotrek, Metal Marines, Umihara Kawase and Sutte Hakkun. Licensed games were considered individually, including Goof Troop, Batman Returns and the Super Star Wars trilogy.

Substantial sequels can each earn a place. Sports coverage uses representative seasons and different play styles rather than every annual edition. Cross-system duplication is not automatically removed: the SNES versions of games also selected on Mega Drive remain eligible on their own merits.

## Release policy

- Prefer an English USA release, then a suitable World/European release, using the latest numbered revision within the chosen region where catalogued.
- Ordinary retail releases are the default. Betas, prototypes, samples, pirate variants, generic multicarts, unverified hacks and fan translations are excluded. Legitimate Japanese imports remain eligible; text-heavy untranslated RPGs are treated separately below.
- This selection uses the ordinary SNES cartridge DAT. Satellaview and Sufami Turbo have separate catalogues and are outside this manifest. Their future manifests can map into a shared emulator destination if appropriate.
- English European releases are retained where relevant, including Terranigma and The Firemen. European timing is not automatically converted to NTSC, and a European release is not treated as a second game alongside its US counterpart.
- Final Fantasy II and Final Fantasy III are the North American cartridge names for Final Fantasy IV and VI. Those names are retained exactly as the DAT defines them. R-Type III uses the USA entry.
- Space Megaforce represents Super Aleste, Firepower 2000 represents Super SWIV, and Tetris Attack represents the regional Panel de Pon variant. Japanese alternate editions are not added as duplicate games.
- Super Mario All-Stars is retained for its remade NES games, with Super Mario World selected separately. The combined All-Stars + World cartridge is omitted. Tetris & Dr. Mario is a deliberate compilation choice; Tetris 2 and Tetris Attack have distinct rules. Kirby's Avalanche and Super Puyo Puyo Tsuu Remix are distinct entries, not two regional copies of the same game.

### Official rerelease exceptions

Two releases intentionally depart from the original-retail preference:

- **Trials of Mana (World) (En) (Rev 1) (Collection of Mana)**: the DAT-catalogued English SNES ROM from the official collection. This is the selected English version of Seiken Densetsu 3; the Japanese original is not also selected. A fan-translated ROM is not an interchangeable checksum match.
- **Star Fox 2 (USA, Europe) (Classic Mini, Switch Online)**: the released version catalogued by No-Intro, rather than an earlier prototype.

These exceptions do not mean all modern reissues or aftermarket software have been included.

## Imports and accessibility

The manifest deliberately includes Japanese-region action and puzzle games. Their main gameplay may be worth exploring without translating every line, but menus, tutorials, progression clues and story can still require outside explanation. No patched translation has been silently selected.

| Goemon and related discoveries | Ganbare Goemon 2 and Kirakira Douchuu; Go Go Ackman 1–3; The Great Battle IV/V; Shounen Ninja Sasuke; Super Back to the Future Part II. Japanese text and minigame instructions remain. |

| Puzzles and competitive play | Tetris Battle Gaiden; Super Puyo Puyo Tsuu Remix; Kirby no Kirakira Kids; Super Bomberman 4/5; Magical Drop 1/2; Wrecking Crew '98; Super Loopz; Lode Runner Twin; Sugoi Hebereke. Extra modes and rules can need explanation. |
| Pinball | Naxat Super Pinball: Jaki Crush and Battle Pinball. Table rules may benefit from a guide. |

The US English versions of SimAnt and R-Type III are selected despite different Japanese titles in the DAT. English availability should be checked before accepting an import as the default.

Text-heavy imports such as Live A Live, Tales of Phantasia, Star Ocean, Bahamut Lagoon, the Romancing SaGa games, Dragon Quest V/VI, Fire Emblem, Tactics Ogre and Front Mission are deferred under the unpatched-ROM policy. Their exclusion is about accessibility and release policy, not an assessment that they lack curation value. Ganbare Goemon 3, Clock Tower, Marvelous and Chaos Seed are also held for a language/translation review.

## Controls and emulator considerations

- Super Scope/light-gun titles such as Yoshi's Safari, Battle Clash, Metal Combat and Super Scope 6 are deferred. Lethal Enforcers, Operation Thunderbolt and Revolution X are likewise outside the current selection pending an appropriate handheld control arrangement.
- Mario Paint and Mario & Wario are deferred for mouse controls. Games with ordinary controller support, including Arkanoid: Doh It Again and Cannon Fodder, do not assume a physical mouse is connected.
- Enhancement-chip games such as Star Fox 1/2, Stunt Race FX, Yoshi's Island, Kirby Super Star, Kirby's Dream Land 3 and Super Mario RPG are selected on their merits, independently of emulator choice.
- Street Fighter Alpha 2, technically ambitious 3D games and some older computer adaptations are retained with their compromises. Original hardware limitations, controls and loading behaviour are not automatically fixed by the selection.
- Multitap play, two-player sessions and optional peripherals are not prerequisites assumed by the manifest. Games such as Super Bomberman and Goof Troop remain worthwhile without guaranteeing multiplayer features on the handheld.

## Research inputs

These informed discovery and balance; they were not copied wholesale or used as rating cutoffs. Cartridge rarity, current prices and collection value were not reasons for inclusion.
