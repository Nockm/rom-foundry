## Why this list is small

This is the original monochrome system. Its Pocket Color successor has a separate DAT and manifest, and supplies most of this family's curated collection. The aim is worthwhile games rather than completing a small catalogue.

| Selection | Reason to retain it |
|---|---|
| King of Fighters R-1 | An earlier handheld interpretation worth exploring alongside R-2, with its own roster and presentation. |
| Samurai Shodown! | A historical companion to the colour sequel: compact fighting with the original monochrome presentation. Less essential than its sequel, but a deliberate curiosity. |

Both selected releases carry English/Japanese DAT language tags. They are distinct earlier games, not extra regional copies of the colour selections. Their historical interest is part of the judgement; this is not a claim that they surpass their sequels.

## Colour editions and holds

Baseball Stars, Pocket Tennis, NeoGeo Cup '98 and Tsunagete Pon! are represented through the corresponding colour releases in the Pocket Color manifest. Puzzle Link is the English title used there for Tsunagete Pon! Color. This keeps the shared family collection focused without automatically eliminating distinct sequels.

Melon-chan no Seichou Nikki is held for a Japanese-language virtual-pet review. Shougi no Tatsujin is held for a deliberate shogi/language choice. Neo Cherry Master is omitted from the default selection; the colour list already has a small card-game selection, and casino catalogue coverage is not a goal.

BIOS images, the combined demonstration, betas and other non-retail variants are excluded. No fan translations or modified ROMs are implied.

## Research inputs

Research-informed editorial judgement, not hands-on testing of every game.
