## Editorial approach

A broad introduction to a compact catalogue, including obvious highlights, genre alternatives and a few worthwhile curiosities. There is no fixed quota or attempt to collect every release. Research supplied leads and different perspectives; inclusion remains editorial judgement, not a claim of hands-on testing or several independent endorsements for every title.

The fighting selection includes Match of the Millennium, The Last Blade, Gals' Fighters, King of Fighters R-2, Samurai Shodown! 2 and Fatal Fury: First Contact. Rockman: Battle & Fighters offers another arcade-fighting interpretation, while Big Bang Pro Wrestling broadens the selection beyond conventional fighters.

Sonic Pocket Adventure and both Metal Slug missions anchor action/platforming. Cotton supplies scrolling shooting; Dark Arms adds action RPG exploration. Biomotor Unitron, Evolution, Faselei! and Card Fighters' Clash provide longer-form play with different battle systems. Dive Alert is a more qualified discovery choice for its submarine premise and unusual pace, rather than an essential recommendation.

Puzzle and arcade choices include Delta Warp, Magical Drop, Puyo Pop, Bust-A-Move Pocket, Puzzle Link 1 and 2, Picture Puzzle, Shanghai Mini, Crush Roller and Pac-Man. Pocket Reversi, Neo 21 and Neo Dragon's Wild add quieter board/card options without filling the list with casino variants.

Neo Turf Masters and Pocket Tennis Color sit alongside both Baseball Stars Color and Dynamite Slugger. The two baseball games are distinct choices. NeoGeo Cup '98 Plus Color and Cool Boarders Pocket provide football and snowboarding; the latter is a modest discovery pick rather than a system-defining highlight. Ganbare Neo Poke-kun adds an eccentric virtual-pet/minigame curiosity.

## Release choices and overlap

- Prefer suitable English USA/World/European retail releases. Japanese releases with explicit English DAT tags remain eligible; region alone is not a language verdict.
- Card Fighters' Clash uses the **SNK Version** as a representative. The Capcom Version is omitted as an overlapping companion edition. Trading-dependent collection goals are optional.
- Dive Alert uses **Matt's Version** as its representative; Becky's Version is deferred as an overlapping alternative.
- Bust-A-Move Pocket represents the game also released as Puzzle Bobble Mini. Puzzle Link represents Tsunagete Pon! Color. Regional aliases are not extra selections.
- Evolution and Pocket Reversi pin European `vA1`; Puyo Pop pins USA `v06`; Neo Dragon's Wild pins World `v13`. These are explicit DAT choices, not a general claim that alphanumeric version labels sort chronologically. Other regional/version copies are omitted.
- The separate monochrome manifest retains KOF R-1 and Samurai Shodown! as distinct earlier games. Colour versions of the sports and Puzzle Link games represent the overlapping monochrome releases instead.
- Ordinary retail ROMs only: no BIOS, prototypes, demos, pirate releases, fan translations or aftermarket additions. Do not rename an unmatched ROM to satisfy a filename.

## Imports and holds

| Selected import | Accessibility consideration |
|---|---|
| Rockman: Battle & Fighters | Japanese-only release; action is the attraction, but menus and explanatory text may need a guide. |
| Ganbare Neo Poke-kun (Kari) | Japanese virtual-pet/minigame oddity. Discovering how its routines and minigames work may require outside instructions. A curiosity with uneven pacing. |

Big Bang Pro Wrestling and Delta Warp are Japanese-region entries with English/Japanese DAT tags; they are not silently patched translations.

Text-heavy untranslated games including Ogre Battle Gaiden, the Unitron sequel, Nige-ron-pa and Card Fighters 2 are held for an explicit language/translation decision. Visual novels and Japanese literacy titles are outside this default selection. Cool Cool Jam is held for language, controls and gameplay review; its unusual musical premise alone does not earn automatic inclusion. Densha de Go! 2 likewise needs a deliberate review of Japanese instructions and train-operation rules.

Slot-machine series, communication utilities and further casino variants are not included merely to make the catalogue complete. These omissions can be revisited if the relevant kind of play becomes a priority.
