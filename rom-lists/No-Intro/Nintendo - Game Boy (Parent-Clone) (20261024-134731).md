## Selection approach

A broad collection for exploration: familiar classics, strong genre games, worthwhile licensed titles, hidden gems and historical curiosities. The selection combines recommendation sources with editorial nominations, rather than applying a single ranking or score cutoff. It was not hands-on testing of every game.

Platforming and adventure are joined by RPGs, puzzle games, pinball, shooters, fighting, sports and racing. Discovery nominations include Amazing Penguin, Bubble Ghost, Catrap, Tail 'Gator, Trax and Legend of the River King. Selected sequels are retained where they provide worthwhile variety; the collection is not limited to one acclaimed entry per series.

## Scope and release choices

- Original monochrome Game Boy software, including Super Game Boy-enhanced releases. Game Boy Color releases belong in their own list, even when backward compatible.
- Prefer suitable English releases and newer numbered revisions when other release details are equivalent.
- Tetris uses World Rev 1 rather than the earlier Japanese release.
- Link's Awakening is the original monochrome game, not the GBC DX edition. Both original and colour interpretations can earn a place on their own merits.
- Pokemon Red and Blue are both retained for version choice. Yellow is also an intended selection. The Donkey Kong Land trilogy and multiple Final Fantasy Legend entries offer worthwhile variety.
- Bomberman GB and Wario Blast are separate selections, not duplicate names for one ROM. Regional naming differences should be checked before future additions.
- Original releases are the default; translations and hacks need an explicit selection policy.

## Additional selections and language decisions

Trip World, Chikyuu Kaihou Gun ZAS, Pokemon Yellow and Wave Race are also intended selections. Their absence from the sibling manifest is not an editorial rejection. ZAS is a particular display/flicker curiosity whose presentation matters to the experience.

Picross 2, Cave Noire, Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru (For the Frog the Bell Tolls), Game Boy Wars Turbo, God Medicine: Hukkoku Han and Another Bible are worthwhile candidates held for a language or translation decision. An untranslated edition is not automatically an accessible default.

## Practical caveats

Ordinary controller play is the baseline. Super Game Boy enhancements and linked multiplayer are optional additions. Navigation-heavy games, older RPGs and some Japanese discoveries may benefit from a manual or guide.
