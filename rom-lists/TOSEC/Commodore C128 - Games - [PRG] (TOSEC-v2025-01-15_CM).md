## Intent and decisions

Two small discoveries supplement the [D64 selection](Commodore%20C128%20-%20Games%20-%20%5BD64%5D%20%28TOSEC-v2025-01-15_CM%29.md): **Mikes Maze** and **Funny Tablets**. This is a curiosity shelf rather than a claim that either rivals the leading C128 action games.

Mikes Maze offers a simple precision-maze challenge. Funny Tablets is a small C128 scene release retained for discovery; its exact controls and appeal need hands-on assessment. Use their ordinary catalogued entries rather than alternate dumps.

Games already selected on D64 are omitted here, including Cross Chase, Maggot, SOKO64+, 4GRAVITY! and the shared 80-column games. In particular, dozens of Cross Chase development builds do not represent dozens of different recommendations. Casino, trivia, educational and text-heavy strategy entries are excluded.

The manifest follows each ROM's actual extension, rather than assuming that everything in a PRG DAT must end in `.prg`. Both selected files are PRG programs. Native C128 mode and keyboard access may be required; inclusion does not establish a ready-made NextUI control mapping.
