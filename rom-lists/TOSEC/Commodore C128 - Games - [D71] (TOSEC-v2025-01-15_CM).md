## Intent and decisions

Two modern interactive-fiction exceptions complement the action and puzzles in the [D64 selection](Commodore%20C128%20-%20Games%20-%20%5BD64%5D%20%28TOSEC-v2025-01-15_CM%29.md): **The Ghosts of Blackwood Manor** and **Hibernated 1 Director's Cut**. They offer contrasting horror and science-fiction settings, with the authors' emphasis on story and approachable puzzle design making them worthwhile introductions to the genre.

Use the exact r13 releases catalogued here. Keep Hibernated's Director's Cut rather than substituting its earlier incarnation. Neither title is repeated in the companion D64 or PRG manifest. The selection is deliberately small: it does not include the rest of the parser catalogue merely because there is room.

Both games require substantial reading and typed commands. These are recommendations for someone willing to use a keyboard or the emulator's keyboard interface, not claims of comfortable gamepad-only play. Use native C128 mode. The authors provide C128 loading instructions; Ghosts also documents optional 80-column operation. Performance and presentation on the Brick remain untested.
