## Selection approach

Broad quality and discovery rather than only the highest-rated games. The list combines familiar recommendations with genre specialists, worthwhile licensed games, unusual ideas and interesting adaptations. It is editorial judgement informed by the sources below, not a claim that every title has multiple endorsements or has been play-tested on the handheld.

The selection covers platforming and exploration, action, licensed adventures, RPGs, strategy, puzzles, arcade games, shooters, fighting, racing and sports. Original handheld games are prominent, but ports remain eligible where they offer a worthwhile portable interpretation. Nothing requires filling a fixed game quota.

Examples of discovery nominations include Car Battler Joe, Rebelstar: Tactical Command, Racing Gears Advance, Scurge: Hive, Wade Hixton's Counter Punch, Kim Possible 2 and Justice League Heroes: The Flash. Licensed titles were considered individually rather than rejected as a category. Games with rough edges can still earn a place for an unusual idea or genre appeal.

## Release policy

- Prefer an English USA retail release, then an appropriate World/European release; use the latest numbered revision within that region where catalogued.
- Availability does not change the selected release. Do not substitute untranslated Japanese RPGs for the selected English releases.
- Exclude prototypes, betas, demos, videos, multiboot downloads, e-Reader-only software, generic compilations that duplicate selected standalone games, and modern reissue variants by default.
- Dr. Mario + Puzzle League is a deliberate compilation exception: both games make a worthwhile combined package. Arcade collections such as Activision Anthology and Namco Museum are included for their different kind of breadth.
- Pokemon Emerald represents Ruby/Sapphire's setting; Ruby and Sapphire are omitted as largely overlapping alternatives. FireRed and LeafGreen are both retained for version choice. For Battle Network, retain selected entries and one representative of paired editions rather than every version; Battle Network 4 is omitted editorially. These are selection decisions, not claims that other versions are invalid.
- Both Friends of Mineral Town editions are retained as distinct protagonist/relationship options. GBA ports of Mario, Donkey Kong Country and other classics remain selected even if a future SNES library also includes originals; cross-system deduplication is not automatic.

## Imports and practical caveats

The following unpatched Japanese releases are selected for gameplay/discovery interest. This is not a claim that their menus, instructions, dialogue or optional modes are English or require no outside explanation:

- Densetsu no Stafy 1, 2 and 3: platforming imports; dialogue and some objectives may need a guide.
- Rhythm Tengoku: rhythm-game discovery; Japanese tutorial instructions may need explanation.
- Kururin Paradise: additional precision-maze challenges, with Japanese menus.
- Mr. Driller A: Japanese menus and extra modes may need a guide.
- Guru Logichamp: Japanese interface; learn its puzzle controls before judging accessibility.
- F-Zero Climax: Japanese menus and features may need a guide.

Text-heavy untranslated games such as Mother 3, Fire Emblem: The Binding Blade, Tomato Adventure and Magical Vacation are deferred. No translation or patched ROM has been silently substituted into the verified-ROM workflow.

Boktai and its sequels, WarioWare: Twisted!, and Yoshi's Universal Gravitation/Topsy-Turvy are deferred until cartridge-sensor emulation and controls are verified for the actual NextUI setup. These are practical holds, not negative quality judgements. Drill Dozer remains included; rumble support should be checked but its absence does not require excluding the game.

A Link to the Past is the single-player reason to include its cartridge; Four Swords multiplayer is not assumed available. Linked play, e-Reader extras and multiplayer unlocks are not assumed to work elsewhere either. The ordinary Super Mario Advance 4 release is selected, without claiming that e-Reader levels are included.
