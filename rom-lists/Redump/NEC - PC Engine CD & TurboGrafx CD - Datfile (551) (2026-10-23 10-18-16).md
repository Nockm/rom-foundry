## Intent

A shooter-rich collection with enough action, puzzles and English-language adventures to show the CD library's range. Japanese action imports are welcome where reading is not central to play; long untranslated narrative games are outside the selection.

For a first handful, start with **Rondo of Blood, Gate of Thunder, Lords of Thunder, Ys Book I & II, Seirei Senshi Spriggan, Star Parodier, The Dynastic Hero** and **Splash Lake**. **Sapphire** is another leading recommendation once its Arcade Card support is confirmed.

These are editorial judgements, not a ranking or a device-tested compatibility list. The percentage compares disc entries, including regional variants and non-game material in the full catalogue; it does not estimate storage savings.

## Release decisions

- Prefer English North American releases when available, and one ordinary catalogued pressing per title. Retain exact Japanese names and manufacturing labels where needed to identify the chosen import. Reprints, alternate dumps and demos are omitted.
- Rondo of Blood uses the original Japanese `Akumajou Dracula X - Chi no Rondo` release, not the different SNES Dracula X. Its story remains Japanese; no translation patch is implied.
- Gate of Thunder uses the USA compilation that also contains Bonk's Adventure and Bonk's Revenge. These are inseparable bonus games on the selected disc, not extra standalone selections to copy again. Ys Book I & II likewise counts as one selected commercial title. Title counts are not a count of every game inside compilations.
- Choose USA Lords of Thunder once, not both it and Japanese Winds of Thunder. Retaining its Sega CD counterpart is a deliberate platform comparison. The Dynastic Hero, R-Type Complete CD, Super Raiden, Bonk III, Buster Bros. and Rainbow Islands also retain worthwhile CD renditions despite overlap elsewhere.
- Use the ordinary NEXZR release instead of automatically adding Summer Carnival '93: NEXZR Special. Puyo Puyo CD Tsuu represents the series rather than retaining both Puyo CD releases. Bomberman: Panic Bomber is a different puzzle game, not a substitute for the HuCard Bomberman games.
- Rayxanber II is a specialist difficulty pick; Rayxanber III is the more inviting starting point. Super Air Zonk, Cosmic Fantasy 2, Shape Shifter and Chou Aniki are discovery or historical-interest choices rather than universal recommendations.
- Spriggan Mark 2 and the Japanese Valis entries are deliberate action-first exceptions: untranslated dialogue and cutscenes remain. The primary Spriggan is a separate vertical shooter. Valis II and III use their English USA editions.
- Ys IV, the Legend of Xanadu games, Tengai Makyou II and other substantial Japanese narrative games are excluded under the current no-fan-translation policy. Their absence is a language decision, not a judgement that they lack merit. English Exile is retained without automatically including its sequel.
- Arcade Card fighters and specialist-control showcases are not added merely to complete genres. Casino games, quiz catalogues, adult-oriented releases, karaoke, promotional discs and modern unlicensed additions are outside this selection.

## Files and deployment

Follow this shortlist manually. There is no companion copy manifest or automated CD build. Keep every track of the chosen disc together and prepare a playable format separately; a CHD filename alone does not establish which Redump edition it contains.

PC Engine CD games share NextUI's PCE destination with HuCard games, but remain a separate catalogue and selection. CD operation needs suitable System Card firmware. Sapphire specifically uses the Arcade Card expansion, so confirm that support in the installed emulator before treating it as ready to play. This is separate from SuperGrafx support.

Loom and Beyond Shadowgate merit more deliberate control setup than the immediate action games. Forgotten Worlds is not included here because its aiming/control tradeoff is less attractive for this particular handheld selection. Japanese menus and story scenes may remain even when the central action is accessible. No blanket full-speed or gamepad-comfort claim is made.
