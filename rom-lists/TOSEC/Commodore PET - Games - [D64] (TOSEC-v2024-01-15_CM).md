## Intent and decisions

**BioTerror!** complements the PRG selection with a modern PET strategy game: manage an epidemic through quarantines, research and limited resources. Its premise makes an interesting contrast to the platform's small action games and demonstrates what an 8K PET can support.

This is an explicit keyboard-heavy exception. The developer documents typed commands and city identifiers; it is a recommendation for someone willing to use the emulator's keyboard interface, rather than a gamepad-only recommendation. It has not been tested on the Brick.

Select the exact catalogued D64 image. It is not duplicated in the PRG manifest. PETSTRIP, the other entry, is strip poker and is excluded under the collection's general preference to omit casino games. There is no need to fill the whole catalogue simply because it contains only two entries.
