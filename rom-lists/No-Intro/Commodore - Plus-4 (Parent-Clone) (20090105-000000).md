## Intent

A small cartridge selection with two contrasting games: action in Jack Attack, and a quieter puzzle diversion in Viduzzles. There is no quota to fill when the available catalogue is this narrow.

The reduction needs context: this DAT contains only five games. Most of its 39 ROM files belong to BIOS sets and utilities. Keeping two games does not mean rejecting 95% of the Plus/4's games, and this list does not represent the machine's much larger tape and disk library.

## The selection

| Game | Why keep it |
|---|---|
| Jack Attack | The main attraction: compact arcade action with a puzzle element, built around positioning and crushing enemies with blocks. Its 64 levels offer something to work through beyond a single score attempt. |
| Viduzzles | A computerised jigsaw puzzle and a deliberate change of pace. A modest inclusion for short, relaxed sessions rather than a claim that it is a defining classic. |

Both selections use the ordinary USA/Europe release in the DAT, with one `.bin` file per game. There are no alternative releases or multipart cartridges to choose between here.

## Deliberate exclusions

- **Atomic Mission**, **Pirate Adventure** and **Strange Odyssey** are text adventures. Their command entry and reading are central to play, so they are held for a keyboard-oriented collection. Historical importance alone does not make them suitable for this handheld selection.
- Calc Plus, Financial Advisor, LOGO, the keyboard tutorial and Octasoft BASIC are applications or learning tools rather than games for this collection.
- BIOS files belong to emulator setup, not the curated game manifest.

The three adventure omissions are a statement about the intended experience, not their quality. A future text-adventure selection could reasonably include them without changing this list's purpose.

## Format and controls

Keep the DAT's `.bin` names exactly. These are cartridge images; renaming them to `.prg` would not convert them into loadable programs. The deployment needs to attach them as Plus/4 cartridges using the emulator's appropriate loading method. VICE documents both smart attachment and explicit cartridge memory slots.

Allow for keyboard actions in menus or startup, even with an otherwise simple game. This curation does not claim that the particular NextUI launcher configuration has been tested. Any container conversion or launch configuration belongs in deployment, while the verified library retains the DAT-matching originals.
