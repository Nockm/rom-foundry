## Intent

A selective Sega CD shelf that gives the add-on its own identity: distinctive RPGs, shooters, expanded action games and a few worthwhile experiments with CD presentation. These are editorial recommendations, not a device-tested compatibility list or an instruction to install everything.

For a first handful, start with **Sonic CD, Popful Mail, Snatcher, Lunar: Eternal Blue, Shining Force CD, Robo Aleste, The Terminator** and **Final Fight CD**. Add **Road Avenger** for a short introduction to its animated FMV games.

The percentage compares selected disc entries with the entire catalogue, which also includes regional variants and non-game material. It is not a storage estimate or a count of unique games across the platform.

## Release decisions

- Prefer English North American retail releases and one exact catalogued pressing per game. Retain labels such as `RE`, `RE2` and `5R, 6R` as identifiers; do not interpret every manufacturing label as evidence of a gameplay improvement. Alternate dumps and regional duplicates are omitted.
- Sonic CD uses the ordinary USA release and its US soundtrack. This is an explicit regional choice; the Japanese/European soundtrack is an alternative listening preference, not another game to include automatically.
- Lunar's original Sega CD releases remain distinct from their later PlayStation remakes. Shining Force CD is retained despite overlap with Game Gear campaigns because it offers a different presentation and additional books.
- Cartridge overlap is deliberate for Earthworm Jim: Special Edition, The Amazing Spider-Man vs. The Kingpin, Batman Returns and Eternal Champions: Challenge from the Dark Side, where added content or substantial changes justify another entry. Ecco, Mickey Mania, Puggsy and the representative sports games retain CD renditions for presentation or variety. This does not extend to every cartridge port.
- Lords of Thunder is a deliberate cross-platform exception alongside any PC Engine CD selection: the two renditions can be compared rather than assumed interchangeable. Neither is declared universally superior.
- Snatcher is included for ordinary-controller play; its optional lightgun support is not a requirement. Dedicated lightgun showcases are excluded. Eye of the Beholder, Dark Wizard and The Secret of Monkey Island are slower interface-heavy exceptions.
- Keep both discs of Night Trap and Dracula Unleashed. Select ordinary Sega CD editions, not releases labelled `Sega CD 32X` or `Mega-CD 32X`. The latter require a different hardware combination and are outside this shortlist.
- FMV inclusion is selective. Road Avenger, Time Gal, Double Switch and Dracula Unleashed offer different play styles; Night Trap earns its place partly as a historical curiosity. Panic! is another deliberate oddity. Inclusion is not a claim that every experiment has aged gracefully.
- Japanese narrative games without an English edition, fan translations, prototypes, unofficial reworkings, educational discs, music-video products and encyclopedias are outside scope. Sports coverage favours NHL '94 and NBA Jam rather than annual duplication.

## Files and deployment

Follow this checklist manually. There is no companion copy manifest or automated Sega CD build: the existing filename copier does not assemble disc tracks, convert images or prepare multi-disc launches.

A CHD filename does not establish a match to the chosen Redump pressing. The DAT describes CUE/BIN layouts; playable disc files, matching regional firmware and any required disc-switching arrangement are prepared separately.

For Shining Force CD, plan backup RAM cartridge support and save management before committing to the campaigns. Its manual distinguishes internal backup memory from cartridge memory; adequate persistent save space is part of the experience, not another game disc to add. Six-button mapping merits attention for Eternal Champions, while interface-heavy adventures may need more deliberate controls. Playback timing and performance on the Brick remain to be tested.
