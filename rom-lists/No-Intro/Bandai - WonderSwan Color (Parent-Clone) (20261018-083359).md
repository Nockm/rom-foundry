## Intent

A compact, import-friendly collection built around Judgement Silversword, Dicing Knight., Mr. Driller, GunPey EX and Guilty Gear Petit 2. Keep enough action, puzzle and fighting games to explore the machine without turning the menu into a catalogue of Japanese RPGs that require substantial translation support.

The 253 entries include revisions, BIOS files, utilities and a large amount of WonderWitch software. They are not 253 distinct commercial games. Each selected game has one ROM file. The monochrome WonderSwan catalogue remains a separate selection, even though the same emulator can run it.

## The selection

| Strand | Games | Why keep them |
|---|---|---|
| Defining exclusives | Judgement Silversword - Rebirth Edition; Dicing Knight. | The main discoveries: concentrated shooting and a distinctive action dungeon crawler. |
| Puzzle anchors | Mr. Driller; GunPey EX; Tetris; XI Little | Different forms of spatial planning and short-session play. |
| Puzzle discoveries | Kurupara!; Raku Jongg | More variety for someone willing to learn an unfamiliar rule set. Raku Jongg is tile-matching rather than a reason to include every mahjong title. |
| Fighting | Guilty Gear Petit 2; Digimon Tamers - Battle Spirit; Battle Spirit - Digimon Frontier; One Piece - Grand Battle Swan Colosseum | Stronger reasons to investigate the licensed action side of the library. The Digimon games use a different approach from a conventional health-bar fighter. |
| Shooting and robot action | RUN=DIM; SD Gundam - Operation U.C. | Additional arcade play without needing to follow the strategy-heavy Gundam releases. |
| Platform and action | Dragon Ball; Rockman EXE WS; Ultraman | Imports whose moment-to-moment play is more accessible than their Japanese RPG counterparts. |
| Rhythm | Rhyme Rider Kerorican | A distinctive change of pace, provided the controls and audio timing suit the setup. |
| Sport | Final Lap Special; Wonder Classic; Kinnikuman II-Sei - Dream Tag Match | Racing, golf and wrestling give the collection some breadth. |
| English role-playing | Digimon - Anode Tamer & Cathode Tamer - Veedramon Version | An official English option for a substantial adventure, rather than assuming a Japanese release is equally approachable. |
| Qualified curiosity | Golden Axe | A familiar game to sample, with modest expectations of this conversion. |

Not every inclusion is an equal recommendation. Final Lap Special is a useful racing option rather than a revelation. Wonder Classic needs some menu learning, and Golden Axe is kept for curiosity and variety. Neither reputation nor scarcity guarantees quality.

## Release and language decisions

- Select **Judgement Silversword - Rebirth Edition (Rev 5C21)**, the verified parent entry in this DAT. Do not duplicate it with Rev 4321 or the earlier WonderWitch version. Rebirth Edition is a commercial cartridge release despite the game's hobbyist-development origins.
- Select **Dicing Knight. (Japan)**, including the period in its title. The developer describes it as an expanded version of the earlier competition game. The separate DAT entry **Dicing Knight (Japan) (En)** is not assumed to be the same release translated into English. Keep the verified commercial Period version and treat it as a guide-friendly Japanese-language exception.
- Select the **Hong Kong English Veedramon Version** of Digimon Anode/Cathode Tamer. Omit the Korean English variant rather than duplicating the adventure.
- Select the **English/Japanese Digimon Tamers - Battle Spirit** and **Frontier Rev 1**. Battle Spirit Ver. 1.5 is held to avoid another overlapping fighter; an expanded roster does not automatically justify a third entry.
- **Guilty Gear Petit 2** represents that series. The first Petit is omitted to keep the stronger sequel as the starting point.
- **Rockman EXE WS** is the action-platforming game, distinct from **Rockman EXE - N1 Battle**. Do not infer play style from the shared licence alone.
- Kurupara uses Rev 1. Keep the ordinary full release of One Piece Grand Battle, not its sample.
- Tetris retains its `.wsc` filename and **WS Compatible** tag. Compatibility with the older machine is not a reason to move it out of its DAT-defined catalogue.

Japanese menus and occasional dialogue remain in most of the action games. They are selected because a short explanation or manual can make their core play approachable, not because everything is in English. Dicing Knight. especially deserves instructions for its items and systems.

Final Fantasy I, II and IV, Riviera, Front Mission, the SaGa games, Atelier, Arc the Lad and the strategy-heavy Gundam titles are held for their reading burden. Their importance is acknowledged; fan translations would be separately managed derived releases. Visual novels and dialogue-led mysteries are held for the same reason.

## Controls and deployment

WonderSwan's two directional button clusters and its portrait/landscape layouts need deliberate mapping. Rotation changes how a game is held, not merely its picture. Judgement Silversword and other portrait-oriented games need a comfortable layout, while Rhyme Rider uses inputs from both button clusters. Keep these games, but do not assume the default mapping suits every one.

Rhythm play also depends on responsive audio and display timing. A poor experience in Rhyme Rider can reflect the setup as well as the game. Save support matters for longer games such as the English Digimon adventure and Dicing Knight.

The NextUI WSC pak documents **Bandai WonderSwan Color (WSC)** as its ROM folder and supports both WonderSwan generations. It is an additional emulator pak; the list does not install it or enable a deployment script entry. Retain the DAT filenames and `.wsc` extensions.

WonderWitch contest programs are not automatically equivalent to ordinary retail cartridge images. Their runtime, packaging and completion status warrant separate review. No WonderWitch demos, prototypes, utilities, flash-cartridge firmware or later homebrew are included here. Cardinal Sins is a worthwhile future candidate for that separate assessment, rather than an unexamined addition to this retail-focused list.
